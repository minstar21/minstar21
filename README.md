# About Me

Hello! I'm a Data Science graduate with an M.S. from **Seoul National University of Science and Technology (SeoulTech)**.

My primary interests lie in **AI Agents, LLM Pre-training and Post-training, Memorization, and Machine Unlearning**.

I am particularly interested in understanding how language models acquire, retain, and forget information, as well as developing reliable AI systems that integrate language models with external tools and structured knowledge.

### Research Interests

- **AI Agents** — Tool-using agents, RAG, and reliable agentic workflows
- **LLM Pre-training & Post-training** — Language model training, fine-tuning, and optimization
- **Memorization & Unlearning** — Measuring memorization, evaluating forgetting, and selective knowledge removal

### Research & Projects

- **LLM Memorization & Unlearning:** Investigating how memorization metrics influence forget-set selection and unlearning outcomes.
- **Evidence-Grounded AI Agents:** Building document-grounded agents with structured evidence retrieval and verification.
- **LLM Reliability:** Exploring training objectives and evaluation methods for reducing hallucinations.
